## 📖 Description

*A group of teenagers is trying to break into an abandoned building in east Germany to plant a bomb. The German police are guarding the place and doing everything they can to stop them. The attackers must fight their way inside, while the defenders hold their ground and protect the building from being blown up.*

---

## 🗺️ About the Map

This map is inspired by legacy versions of **Overpass**, **Vertigo**, and **Nuke**. It's a very fast-paced but also a bit complicated map.

It was not intended to be well-optimized or competitively oriented — the main focus was on **fun** and **gameplay quirks/gimmicks**.

---

## ⚠️ Easter Egg Warning

This map contains a few easter eggs, **one of which has a jumpscare**. If you are sensitive to such things, please don't go into dark and hidden places on this map! 🙂
